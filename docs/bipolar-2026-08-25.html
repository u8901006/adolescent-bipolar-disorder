BMJ open · MELODY trial: study protocol for a 12-week randomised controlled trial of adjunctive melatonin, digital cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia or pill placebo to improve depressive symptoms in young adults with mood disorders.

Schizophrenia bulletin · Suicidal Ideation and Non-suicidal Self-injury Following Childhood Psychotic Experiences in Adolescents at Familial High Risk of Schizophrenia or Bipolar Disorder-The Danish High Risk and Resilience Study, VIA 15.
